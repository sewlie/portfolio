01 / WHO WE ARE NEW YORK CANNABIS LEAGUE

MORE THAN
A GAME.

A new way to bring the New York cannabis community together.

NYCL is an initiative built around the idea that the people behind the industry deserve a space to connect outside of work.

Through soccer, friendly competition, and shared experiences, we're working to create something that brings dispensaries, industry professionals, and brands together.

We're starting with an inaugural tournament and seeing where it takes us.
