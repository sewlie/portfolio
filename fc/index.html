01THE IDEA

MORE THAN A MATCH

A LEAGUE OF
OUR OWN.

A city full of neighborhoods. A city full of footballers. A league that brings them together.

FOUNDING XI is a working name for a proposed NYC 7v7 indoor football league. The concept: each participating licensed dispensary backs a team, with a season built around competition, community, and a city-wide final.

We're gathering interest from players, potential team operators, venues, and prospective partners. The founding-season details will be announced after the league structure and venue are confirmed.
