01 / WHO WE ARE NEW YORK CANNABIS LEAGUE

MORE THAN
A GAME.

A new way to bring the New York cannabis community together.

NYCL is an initiative started by two dispensary workers who love to play soccer. We're building a fun, non-competitive space for industry workers to connect and play some footy!

Brands are coming in to support the NYCL so the players can focus on having fun. Whether you're playing or supporting, this league will surely bring out the best of New York's cannabis industry.
